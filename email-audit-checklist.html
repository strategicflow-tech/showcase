The 7-Point
Email Audit.

Score 1 point per check. 7/7 = conversion-ready. Below 5 = rebuild before sending. Use on any email — product update, newsletter, lifecycle, outreach.

Verdict
7/7 — Ship it 6/7 — Fix one thing 5/7 — Needs work Below 5 — Rebuild
01
Subject Line
Does it create a curiosity gap or name a specific consequence? Or does it announce the topic like a folder label?
Filing Label Subject
02
Lead Construction
Does the first sentence name a consequence, outcome, or reader problem? Or does it open with context, a caveat, or "we are excited to announce"?
Consequence-After-Caveat
03
Feature-to-Outcome Translation
Does the body name what changes for the reader? Or does it describe what the product does without saying what the reader gains?
Feature-First Bias
04
Visual Hierarchy
Does the most important claim appear above the fold? Or does everything look equally important with no clear priority?
Missing Hierarchy
05
Before / After Contrast
Is the transformation explicit? Does the email name what the reader's life looked like before and what changes after?
No Transformation Named
06
Social Proof
Is there a third-party voice — a named customer, a specific result, a number — or is every claim made by the brand about itself?
Zero Social Proof
07
CTA Language
Does the CTA name what the reader owns or accomplishes? Or does it use guest language — "Learn more", "Click here", "Explore"?
Generic Urgency Theatre
