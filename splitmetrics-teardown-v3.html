Strategic Flow — SplitMetrics Teardown

A structural teardown of a SplitMetrics email campaign analyzed through the Strategic Flow Method. This teardown examines the email's subject line architecture, lead hierarchy, feature framing, and CTA momentum against 7 diagnostic checks used across 54 SaaS email audits.
