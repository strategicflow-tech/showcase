01 · The Method

Make the friction visible.

The Decision Friction Model turns vague “low engagement” into named structural checkpoints you can inspect, rewrite, and test.

Seven checkpoints. One reader decision.

Strategic Flow studies what happens after an email is opened: where the reader loses the thread, doubts the outcome, or is asked to act as a guest in someone else’s story. The model was shaped across 59 real SaaS email teardowns.

05 · FAQ

Short answers, clear next step.

The model is rigorous. The explanation should not be.

What is Strategic Flow Tech?
Strategic Flow Tech is a SaaS lifecycle email audit and rebuild system based on the Decision Friction Model, a 7-point framework built from 59 teardown analyses.
What is the Decision Friction Model?
It checks seven recurring sources of hesitation: Feature-First Bias, Filing Label Subject, Guest Language CTA, Consequence-After-Caveat, Missing Visual Hierarchy, Zero or Buried Social Proof, and Generic Urgency Theatre.
Is Strategic Flow free?
Yes. The four core tools are free to use, and the Audit App shows a live score before asking for an email.
What does the Audit App do?
It scores an email from 1 to 10 across five active signals: subject, hook, feature-to-outcome translation, proof, and CTA ownership.
What is the difference between the Audit App and the Demo?
The Audit App is the live audit tool. The Demo is a guided preview of the same kind of diagnostic flow.
What is WHY.?
WHY. is a general content-friction diagnostic for emails, landing pages, proposals, and other content where a reader needs to take a specific action.
What is ToolIndex?
ToolIndex is a U.S.-focused SaaS and AI tool directory with more than 1,300 listings and a free dofollow backlink for eligible submissions.
How many teardowns has Strategic Flow published?
Strategic Flow has published 59 teardown analyses, with an average score lift from 3.4 out of 10 to 9.0 out of 10.
What is the most common email failure?
Guest Language CTA is the most common observed failure pattern, appearing in 96% of the audited SaaS emails.
Who is Strategic Flow for?
It is for any organization with recurring email communication, with SaaS as the primary audience and applications for media, e-commerce, agencies, and education.
Can I connect through an AI agent?
Yes. The public MCP server at mcp.strategicflow.tech supports Streamable HTTP without authentication and exposes ask_strategic_flow and audit_email.
Who built Strategic Flow?
Strategic Flow was built by Alex Iliescu in Tenerife for a U.S.-focused market.
