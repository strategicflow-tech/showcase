❌ Before

Hook: "One bad AI mention can outweigh 100 good ones."

Mechanism-first. Describes how AI works before naming the reader's problem. A marketing leader whose brand is already cited negatively in ChatGPT gets no signal this is about their situation in line 1.

✅ After

Hook: "Right now, ChatGPT may be describing your brand using a Reddit complaint from 2023. You won't know unless you check."

Reader is the subject. "Right now" creates urgency without a fake deadline. "You won't know unless you check" names the gap that makes the CTA necessary.

The 5 upgrades — and why they work
1 · Hook rebuilt from mechanism to reader situation
The original opens with a claim about AI behavior. The rebuild opens with the reader in the scene — their brand, a specific source type (Reddit complaint), a specific time gap (2023). The reader whose brand has this problem recognises it immediately. The reader whose brand doesn't is still curious enough to check.
2 · Hypothetical scenario replaced with second-person consequence
"A user asks ChatGPT..." is a constructed narrative about someone else. "Most brands find out during a sales call" is a consequence the reader can picture happening to them. Second-person consequence converts better than third-person scenario because the reader is already in the scene before they decide to keep reading.
3 · Dashboard data moved above the fold as stat cards
The original buries the dashboard screenshot at the bottom, after the CTA. The rebuild surfaces three numbers above the fold: 729 mentions, 70% of narratives trace to 3 source types, 6 weeks to shift a signal. Numbers in stat cards register faster than numbers in prose. A reader scanning in 3 seconds sees the scale of the problem before reading a word of body copy.
4 · Features reorganised by problem, not by capability name
The original checklist labels features by what they do: "Monitor how AI engines talk about your brand." The rebuild labels sections by the problem each solves: "The monitoring problem," "The source problem," "The competitor gap." A reader with a specific situation navigates directly to the relevant section instead of reading a capability inventory.
5 · CTA rebuilt with a specific first action, not an outcome category
"Fix your negative narratives" names a category of outcome. "Check what AI says about my brand" names the first action — the thing that happens in the next 30 seconds. Specific, low-friction, ownership language. The reader knows exactly what they are clicking for and what they will see when they arrive.
