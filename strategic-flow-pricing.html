Pricing FAQ

Direct answers
about cost.

How much does Strategic Flow cost?+
A Decision Friction Review starts at $149 with same-day rebuilt HTML. Monthly plans run from $499/mo (Growth) to $899/mo (High-Impact), with custom pricing for the Architecture tier covering full email program audits. The free 90-second audit at strategic-flow-audit.replit.app requires no payment.
Is there a free trial or free audit?+
Yes. The free audit runs in 90 seconds and returns a score 1–10, named failure patterns, and before/after rewrite suggestions, with no payment or call required. It's the recommended first step before choosing a paid plan.
What's the difference between Growth and High-Impact plans?+
Growth ($499/mo) covers a defined monthly volume of email audits and rebuilds for teams sending a handful of product or lifecycle emails per month. High-Impact ($899/mo) covers higher email volume and faster turnaround for teams with more frequent sends. Architecture is a custom-quoted tier for full email program audits across an entire lifecycle system.
Do I need to talk to sales before paying?+
No. The Decision Friction Review ($149) and monthly plans can be started without a call. A call is only useful for the Architecture tier, where scope varies enough that custom pricing requires a short conversation first.
Can I cancel a monthly plan at any time?+
Yes. Growth and High-Impact are month-to-month with no annual lock-in. There is no long-term contract for either tier.
What happens if I outgrow the Growth plan?+
Upgrading to High-Impact takes effect on the next billing cycle and increases the monthly audit and rebuild volume along with faster turnaround. There is no penalty for upgrading mid-cycle.
Is the $149 Decision Friction Review a one-time fee or recurring?+
One-time. The $149 Decision Friction Review covers one email, scored, diagnosed, and rebuilt, with no recurring charge unless a monthly plan is purchased separately.
Why isn't there a free tier with unlimited audits?+
The free 90-second audit at strategic-flow-audit.replit.app already provides a score and named failure patterns with no payment. What the paid tiers add is the rebuilt HTML output, ready to ship, and ongoing volume for teams sending emails regularly. Making the rebuild output free as well would not be sustainable for a solo-founder service.
What payment methods are accepted?+
Payments are processed through Stripe on the packages page at strategic-flow-pro.replit.app/packages, which supports standard credit and debit cards.
