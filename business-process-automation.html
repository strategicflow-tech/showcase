In short

A system that brings order to your everyday business

Today messages arrive by WhatsApp, email and phone; orders live in notebooks and spreadsheets; invoices and reminders depend on someone’s memory. We build a custom system that brings all of that together and automates the repetitive parts:

  1. One inbox and one record per customer. Messages, quotes, orders and documents in the same place.
  2. Replies in minutes, also after hours. An AI assistant handles the simple things and alerts the right person.
  3. From quote to payment with no lost paperwork. Quotes, orders, invoices and reminders prepared from the customer record.
  4. Reminders that don’t get forgotten. Appointments, deliveries, renewals and pending tasks reach the right person on their own.
  5. You are in charge. Nothing important goes out without your approval; everything is logged.

We don’t replace your invoicing software or your way of working: we connect to what you already use and remove the manual steps. The examples on this page are invented; your real case is measured in the initial check.

The problem

Where hours and customers get lost

You don’t need a messy business for this to happen. With many channels and little time, it happens almost everywhere. These are five points along a customer’s journey.

  1. 1. The enquiry arrives

    A WhatsApp at night, an email on the phone, a missed call while you serve someone else.

    What can be lostThe enquiry nobody answered in time. The customer writes to the next business.

  2. 2. Quote or appointment

    The same details are requested again and again; dates are agreed through scattered messages.

    What can be lostHours of back-and-forth and appointments where something is missing.

  3. 3. The order or the job

    Paper notes, spreadsheets, chats; everyone records it their own way.

    What can be lostA detail of the order, or half an hour looking for it.

  4. 4. Deliveries and reminders

    Dates in a diary, on paper, in someone’s head.

    What can be lostA reminder on time: a late delivery, an unbilled renewal.

  5. 5. Invoice and payment

    Invoices pending “until Friday”, reminders written by hand.

    What can be lostMoney to invoice or to chase in time.

How much your business loses, we don’t know yet. We measure it in the initial check, with real cases.

The AI agent

An assistant that works within your rules

The agent always introduces itself as an automated assistant and offers to connect the customer with a person at any time. At first, all its messages go through a human. It is given more freedom only once the team has seen it work, and every message is logged.

What it does on its own

  • Greets the customer and collects basic details.
  • Asks for the documents and photos on the approved list.
  • Offers calendar slots.
  • Answers frequent questions with texts you approved.
  • Creates the record and alerts the person in charge.
  • Reminds the customer of the appointment or delivery.

What it asks you to approve

  • Prices, discounts and terms.
  • Quotes and contracts.
  • Messages to groups of customers.
  • Any complaint.
  • Changes to delivery dates.
  • Anything it cannot find in your approved texts (it says “I’ll check with the team”).

What it never does

  • Promise prices or deadlines on its own.
  • Accept an order or a payment without confirmation.
  • Give medical, legal or tax advice.
  • Make up data: if it doesn’t know, it says so.
  • Keep talking when the customer asks for a person.
  • Use one customer’s data for another.

It stops with a single button. Conversations pass to the team instantly.

Data and security

Your data is yours

A system that handles customer data has to be careful. This is what we do and what we don’t.

What we do

  • The data belongs to your business. Where it is stored and who can access it is agreed in writing before we start.
  • A data processing agreement with every provider that touches data (GDPR, Art. 28).
  • We look for hosting in the European Union whenever possible. We have not verified which provider will be used; it is decided with you.
  • We choose an AI service and plan that does not use your data to train models, and confirm it in writing.
  • Access per person, with two-step verification and permissions per area.
  • A log of who saw or changed what, and backups.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t share customer data with third parties or other businesses.
  • We don’t use real data in tests without your permission.
  • We don’t let the assistant answer on delicate topics without review.
  • We don’t keep more than needed or for longer than agreed.
  • We don’t promise there is “no risk”: we reduce it and document it.

This is not legal advice on data protection. Your business decides with its adviser or data protection officer. In sectors with health data or professional secrecy, the rules are stricter and are reviewed before starting.

Appendix

Glossary: plain words for each term

Automated assistant / AI agent

An artificial intelligence program that answers messages and prepares tasks within the rules you set. It always introduces itself as an automated assistant.

Single inbox

One list where all messages (WhatsApp, email, web) land, instead of being spread across several places.

Record

The page for a customer or an order, with their details, messages, documents and next step.

Draft

Prepared text that is not final yet. Nothing important is sent without a person approving it.

Module

A piece of the system with a specific function (customer service, quotes, invoicing…). Bought separately.

WhatsApp Business Platform

WhatsApp’s official service for businesses. It allows approved templates and automated assistants that identify themselves as such.

Platform

The servers and services where the system lives (hosting, database, backups). It has a fixed monthly fee.

Usage

What AI and messaging providers charge for each use. Passed on at real cost, with a cap.

Connection (integration)

A link between the system and another program you already have, so data isn’t copied by hand.

GDPR

The European regulation on the protection of personal data.

Data processor

A company that processes data on behalf of another. It must sign a specific contract (Art. 28 GDPR).

Two-step verification

A second check when signing in (for example, a code on your phone), in addition to the password.

Activity log

The list of who did what and when inside the system. Used for review and correction.

Phase 0 / initial check

Two days at the start: we measure with real cases where time is lost and prepare a fixed quote.
