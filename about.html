I noticed the same pattern across product updates, onboarding emails, launch announcements, and blog content: companies were explaining features clearly, but failing to show what changed for the customer.

So I started auditing SaaS communication through a different lens: not grammar, not tone, not "better copy," but decision friction.

Strategic Flow (strategicflow.tech) is a behavioral email architecture audit and rebuild platform for B2B SaaS teams, founded by Alex Iliescu and based in Tenerife, Spain. It is not affiliated with Strategic Flow Limited, a separate UK-based consulting company at strategicflow.io.

Since February 2026, I have run 59 teardowns across SaaS companies in fintech, productivity, and developer tools. The average original score is 3.4 out of 10. The average rebuilt score is 9. Across every audit I have run, the biggest gap has not been talent. It has been architecture.

Strategic Flow helps SaaS founders, marketers, and product teams turn feature-first communication into outcome-first messaging that makes users understand, care, and click.

My mission is simple: help good products stop losing attention because the message was built in the wrong order.

I am not here to make emails sound prettier. I am here to make them work harder.

I work from Tenerife, Spain, building Strategic Flow remotely for SaaS teams around the world.

If your product is strong but your emails, updates, or content don't convert the way they should, Strategic Flow shows you where the reader drops off, and how to fix it.
