Diagnostic

What changed & why

Five structural upgrades. Each one addresses a specific bug in the original.

❌ Before
Regulatory, Analytics & Investor Communications Solutions for the investment management industry.
Category description. Three solution areas listed. No consequence named. Indistinguishable from any enterprise data vendor in the space.
✅ After
Your regulatory deadlines don't move. Your data processes shouldn't either.
Names the operational tension every compliance officer at an asset manager feels. The deadline is fixed. The process is the variable. Confluence removes the friction between the two.
1
Title: replace category description with operational consequence
"Regulatory, Analytics & Investor Communications Solutions for the investment management industry" is the kind of headline that could sit on the homepage of 20 competitors. It describes what Confluence offers. It does not name what breaks without it. A compliance officer managing regulatory filings across 40 jurisdictions manually knows exactly what the consequence is: missed deadlines, data errors, and teams working weekends before every filing date. "Your regulatory deadlines don't move. Your data processes shouldn't either." names the tension directly. It creates recognition before the buyer reads a single feature description.
2
Rotating word animation removed — consequence replaces decoration
"Expect service integrity discipline imagination" cycling through words is a brand values animation, not a hook. Brand values earn the right to be stated after the buyer understands what the product does for them — not before. The animation occupies the highest-attention real estate on the page and delivers zero information about the buyer's situation. Every second a buyer spends watching words rotate is a second they haven't yet understood why Confluence solves their problem. The rebuild removes the animation entirely and leads with the operational consequence instead.
3
Proof reframed: "30 years" becomes competitive evidence, not tenure
"30+ years of experience" tells the buyer Confluence is old. It does not tell them what that experience means for a firm evaluating them today. The rebuild reframes the same numbers as competitive evidence: 1,000+ firms trusted Confluence with their most regulated workflows, the top 10 asset servicers globally chose them, and 40+ countries means they have already solved the jurisdiction problem the buyer is about to encounter. The stat "10/10 top asset servicers" — which appears in Confluence's own client page but not on the homepage — is the most specific proof point available and belongs in the hero zone.
4
Segment cards: replace generic one-liners with role-specific failure states
Four segment cards with four identical-weight descriptions — "streamline," "gain efficiency," "increase accuracy," "automate" — tell the buyer what Confluence does but not what they are suffering without it. An asset manager does not search for a vendor to "streamline regulatory reporting." They search because a junior analyst spent 60 hours preparing a regulatory filing that should have taken 6. The rebuild replaces each generic one-liner with the specific failure mode that drives each segment to evaluate Confluence. Recognition drives engagement. Generic descriptions drive bounces.
5
Solutions list: outcome framing replaces feature inventory
Six solution items listed at equal weight with short descriptors that name the function but not the outcome. "Regulatory Reporting & Filing — accuracy across global jurisdictions" describes what the product does. The rebuilt version leads each solution section with the operational consequence — "Regulatory reporting that files itself across 40+ jurisdictions" — and names specifically what the team member responsible no longer has to do. The compliance officer, the performance analyst, the investor communications team each have a section that names their problem before it names the solution. Consequence-first architecture always outperforms feature-first architecture for enterprise buyers with specific, painful problems.

This is the Strategic Flow method

Every word earns its place. The reader's consequence leads — not a brand values animation. Proof is reframed as competitive evidence, not tenure. Each segment sees their specific failure state named, not a generic one-liner. Solutions lead with what the buyer no longer has to do, not what the product does.
