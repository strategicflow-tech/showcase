How this is read, and what's scored

Two separate drafts, scored independently, then compared. Each is read once as a cold recipient would read it, checked against four structural points. No rewrite is included. This is diagnosis only.

All 7 points of the Decision Friction Model, run against both drafts. No point skipped, no partial framework. Diagnosis only, no rebuild, that's a separate deliverable.

01Subject lineFiling label or consequence signal? A DM has no subject field, so the opening line is scored as its structural equivalent.
02Lead constructionConsequence before caveat, or context before consequence?
03Feature-to-outcome translationDoes it name what changes for the reader, or what the product does?
04Visual hierarchyDoes the most important claim lead, or is it buried, or is there no hierarchy at all?
05Proof placementIs the strongest stat, quote, or named result above the fold, or absent entirely?
06Social proofIs there a third-party voice, a number, a named result?
07CTA languageOwnership language ("reply run") or guest language, or no true CTA at all?

Failure pattern vocabulary

Every fail below is one of these exact patterns, no paraphrasing. Only the five that actually appear in these two drafts are listed here.

→Feature-First BiasThe message leads with what the product does, not what the reader gains. Triggered when point 03 fails: mechanism described, no outcome named.
→Consequence-After-CaveatThe outcome is buried behind context, disclaimers, or qualifications. Triggered when point 02 fails: the message concedes ground or sets context before it states its claim.
→Missing HierarchyNo structural priority. Everything looks equally important. Triggered when point 04 fails: no line, claim, or word is weighted above the others.
→Zero Social ProofNo third-party voice, no named result, no specific number. Triggered when points 05 and 06 fail together: nothing in the message proves the claim has ever worked, only that it's being made.
→Guest Language CTAThe CTA describes what the brand offers, not what the reader does, or no true CTA exists at all. Triggered when point 07 fails: the reader has no owned action, only an offer or an opt-out.
