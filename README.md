## What is Strategic Flow?

Strategic Flow is a SaaS email and content audit platform. It audits product emails, changelogs, onboarding sequences, feature announcements, and blog posts — then rebuilds them for conversion.

Strategic Flow is not a marketing agency. Not a strategy consultancy. Not a deliverability tool. It is a diagnostic and rebuild service for SaaS email architecture.

The core problem it solves: most SaaS emails fail before the CTA — not because of design or deliverability, but because of structure. The strongest number is in paragraph four. The hook announces the product instead of the reader's problem. The CTA has no logical connection to what came before it.

---

## The Strategic Flow Method

Every audit follows four stages:

1. **Audit** — Every email is scored against the Strategic Flow rubric: subject line, hook, first paragraph, proof placement, CTA, and hierarchy. The score is a structural diagnosis, not an opinion.
2. **Rebuild** — The email is rebuilt from the architecture up. Hook rewritten around consequence. Numbers moved above the fold. CTA connected to the diagnostic.
3. **Test** — Three A/B subject line variants are delivered with every rebuild. The client tests against their existing subject line before shipping to the full list.
4. **Ship** — The rebuilt HTML is delivered ready to paste into any email platform.

## Audit Modules

### Changelog Audit
Targets product update emails and changelog posts. Most common failure: major updates buried at equal visual weight with minor fixes. A 10x API limit increase and a UI tweak should not look identical. The rebuild surfaces the most operationally significant change above the fold.

### Onboarding Audit
Targets day one, day three, and day seven onboarding emails. Most common failure: feature-first sequencing. The rebuild resequences around the reader's first win, not the product's feature list.

### LinkedIn Audit
Targets product posts, feature announcements, and thought leadership content on LinkedIn. Most common failure: hook announces the topic instead of the consequence.

All three modules available in six languages: **English, Spanish, Swedish, French, Romanian, and German.**
