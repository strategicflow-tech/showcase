Who we are

Somatization Map is published by Strategic Flow, run by Alex Iliescu, Adeje, Tenerife, Spain. Contact: strategicflow@proton.me.

What this is

Somatization Map is an educational reflection tool. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose and does not replace your doctor, psychologist or therapist. See a doctor first, especially for physical symptoms. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services (112 in the EU).

Who can buy

The paid programs are for adults. By buying you confirm that you are 18 or over.

Programs, prices and payment

The free guide is free and needs no account. Each paid 7-day program costs 9 € and the pack of four programs costs 29 €. Each is a single payment: there is no subscription and no automatic renewal. The final price, including any taxes that apply, is shown at checkout before you confirm. Payment is processed by Stripe, which provides your receipt or invoice.

What you get and for how long

A program is a private web page with seven daily steps. All seven days are available from the moment of purchase, so you can go at your own pace and repeat any day. There is no limit on devices. The page works in a browser and is not a downloadable file; your own notes can be copied with the copy button. The pack gives you one private page with links to all four programs. Your access lasts at least 12 months from the purchase date and, in any case, for as long as the program is offered. If we ever plan to withdraw a program, we will email buyers at least 60 days in advance.

Delivery and confirmation

After payment you are taken to your private program page and we email you the same link from noreply@strategicflow.tech. That email also confirms, on a durable medium, your request for immediate access and your acknowledgement that the right of withdrawal ends once access starts. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder or write to strategicflow@proton.me and we will resend it.

Right of withdrawal

Under EU consumer law you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a distance purchase. For digital content that is supplied immediately, you give up this right only if you expressly ask for immediate access and acknowledge that you lose the right of withdrawal once access starts. At checkout you do this with a separate box that you must tick yourself; without it you cannot complete the purchase. Your other legal rights as a consumer are not affected.

Technical problems and your consumer rights

If you have trouble accessing a program, or if digital content you bought does not match the description shown when you bought it, write to strategicflow@proton.me. We will review your request and apply the remedies provided by consumer protection law, including bringing the content into conformity, reducing the price, or ending the contract with a refund, where the legal conditions are met. As an extra, we give priority support to problems reported within the first 30 days. This extra does not limit your legal rights.

Your private link and personal use

Your program link is private and for your own use. Please do not publish or resell it. If we notice that a link is widely shared, we will contact you and may issue you a new link. We will not withdraw access from a legitimate buyer without telling you first and giving you the chance to explain.

Complaints

To make a complaint, write to strategicflow@proton.me with your name, the email used for the purchase and a description of the problem. We keep a written record of complaints and our replies, and we aim to answer within 5 business days. You can also contact the consumer protection authority of your country or region.

Privacy: who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Strategic Flow, run by Alex Iliescu (contact: strategicflow@proton.me). We collect only what is needed for the purposes below.

Privacy: what we collect and why

Free guide by email: your email address, your chosen language and the time and text of your consent, to send you the guide link. Legal basis: your consent. We keep it until you ask us to delete it, and we do not use it for marketing. Purchases: your email address and payment status (Stripe passes these to us), a record of which program was delivered and when, and your request for immediate access, to deliver the program and prove delivery and consent. Legal basis: performing the contract and, for invoices and tax records, legal obligations; we keep them for the period required by tax and consumer law. Support messages: what you write to us, to answer you. Legal basis: performing the contract or our legitimate interest in answering you; we keep them for as long as needed to handle your request and for legal claims. We do not ask for health data. Please do not write identifying details or health information in your notes.

Privacy: your notes stay in your browser

Your check-ins, tracker entries and journal notes are saved in your own browser (local storage) so that the tools work, and they are never sent to us. They are strictly necessary for the function you use, so no consent banner is needed for them. You can delete them at any time with the delete button on the page or by clearing this site's data in your browser. This site uses no analytics or advertising cookies. Privacy: your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete the data we hold about you, restrict or object to its use, ask for portability, and withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to strategicflow@proton.me. Educational content only. The Somatization Map does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified health professional about your symptoms.
