The Problem

Your onboarding was written by the wrong people, for the wrong goal.

Product teams write about features. Users activate because of consequences. The gap between those two things is where your trial-to-paid conversion lives — and dies.

Failure Pattern 01
Feature-First Bias
Day 1 email explains what the product does. It never names what changes for the user after they complete setup. The reader finishes knowing more about your product and still having no reason to log back in.
Failure Pattern 02
The Activation Gap
There is always a moment between "user signed up" and "user got value." Most sequences skip the email that bridges that gap. Nobody wrote it. Nobody noticed it was missing. The user dropped off silently.
Failure Pattern 03
CTA Fatigue Across Channels
Email: "Start your setup." In-app: "Set up your account." Day 3: "Complete your onboarding." Three messages. Same verb. Zero progression. The reader learned to ignore all of them.
Failure Pattern 04
No Consequence Named
The entire sequence describes actions. None of them name what happens if the user doesn't act. No stakes. No urgency. No reason today is different from tomorrow.
