Lodgify Named Airbnb Preferred+ Software Partner for 2026
We're excited to announce that Lodgify has been selected as an Airbnb Preferred+ Partner for 2026! Lodgify has had a fully functioning, two-way connection with Airbnb for several years, and we have continued to expand our integration to achieve the deepest connectivity possible.
Our commitment to providing outstanding channel management services has led to accolades such as Airbnb Preferred+ partnership status, as well as premier partnership status with Vrbo, Booking.com, and Expedia. The recent achievement of Airbnb Preferred+ Partner places us among the top 18 providers globally who have met and exceeded Airbnb's rigorous standards.
What Preferred+ status means for our users
"This recognition confirms Lodgify's status as a top-tier software integration." — Sofia Vierci, Channel Product Manager, Lodgify
As a Preferred+ Partner, Lodgify enjoys benefits that include fast-tracked and dedicated technical support from Airbnb's API support team. We will also have the opportunity to provide feedback on API features and enjoy other benefits that enhance our status — all perks that help us improve our product for our users.
Being named as one of only 18 Preferred+ Partners is a testament to Lodgify's commitment to excellence, secure practices, and our unwavering dedication to supporting our clients in their hosting journey. We are incredibly proud of this achievement and look forward to leveraging this partnership to create enhanced experiences for our users.

What Preferred+ means in practice
Faster support. Earlier features. No surprises at sync.
Preferred+ is Airbnb's highest tier for software partners. It grants dedicated technical support from Airbnb's API team — not the general queue. It means Lodgify engineers work directly with Airbnb on integration issues before they become visible to hosts. When something changes in Airbnb's system, Lodgify knows first.
The network behind your listings
Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com, Expedia — all at premier level.
Lodgify holds premier partnership status with every major OTA simultaneously. That is not a common position. Most software tools prioritise one channel. Managing four at the highest integration tier means one calendar, one dashboard, and one sync that does not break when two bookings arrive at once.
What this changes for you
The integration is not new. The access level is.
The two-way Airbnb connection has been live for years. What Preferred+ changes is the layer underneath it — API feedback rights, fast-tracked issue resolution, and early feature access. If Airbnb ships a new tool for hosts, Lodgify users see it before the broader market. That gap compounds over time.
Next step
Your Airbnb integration is already running. Make sure it is running at the right level.
If your listings are still managed through a standard integration or a tool without Preferred+ access, the difference shows up in sync reliability, support speed, and feature availability. It is not dramatic until it is.

The 6 upgrades — and why they work
1 · Title reframes from company achievement to host outcome
The original celebrates Lodgify. The rebuild tells the host what changed in their business. A host managing five properties does not care that Lodgify won an award — they care that their sync is faster and their support is better. The title earns the read by answering the reader's actual question first.
2 · "18 globally" reframed from a brag to a signal
The original uses "top 18 globally" as social proof for Lodgify. The rebuild uses the same number as a signal of scarcity and access: most tools are not in this tier, and the difference is operational. Same stat, different angle, different conversion value.
3 · Four stat cards replace the credential paragraph
The original lists achievements in prose. The rebuild surfaces four numbers that answer the host's real questions: how rare is this, how long has the integration been proven, and what does early access actually mean. Numbers above the fold anchor the business case before the explanation begins.
4 · "Not a badge" pre-empts the reader's scepticism
Partnership announcements read as marketing. The rebuild names that scepticism directly — "that is not a badge" — and immediately delivers the operational translation: faster sync, dedicated support, early feature access. Acknowledging the obvious objection before the reader forms it builds credibility faster than ignoring it.
5 · Four OTA partnerships reframed as infrastructure, not a list
The original lists partner logos. The rebuild explains why holding all four at premier level simultaneously is structurally unusual and what it means operationally — one calendar, no double bookings, no sync that breaks under load. Features become architecture.
6 · CTA connected to the announcement's consequence
"Start free trial" in the original has no logical connection to the Preferred+ news. The rebuild closes with "Connect your Airbnb listings" — a direct consequence of reading about what the integration now delivers. The action follows naturally from the content instead of interrupting it.
