Decision Friction Model
7 behavioral checks. Every email.

Each check maps to a specific reader behavior that determines whether your email gets opened, read, or acted on. Not style. Not tone. Decision friction.

01
Subject line
Filing label or consequence? The subject line that announces the product gets ignored. The one that names the reader's problem gets opened.
02
Hook strength
First line names the reader's consequence, not the company's news. Most SaaS emails open with "We're excited to announce." That is where they lose the reader.
03
Visual hierarchy
Does the most important update get the most visual weight? Flat hierarchy forces the reader to decide what matters. Most readers don't.
04
Numbers above the fold
Specific numbers answer silent objections. "40% faster" answers "is this worth switching?" before the reader has to ask it.
05
CTA ownership language
"Learn more" describes what you're offering. "Fix my reporting" describes what the reader is doing. One gets clicked. The other gets ignored.
06
Activation friction
Onboarding steps that describe actions without naming consequences create drop-off. Each step should name what it unlocks, not just what to do.
07
Audience signal
Line 1 speaks to everyone. That is why it reaches no one. The email that names a specific reader role in the hook converts at a different rate than the one that doesn't.

Common Questions
Before you commit.

Everything you need to know before running your first audit.

What exactly does a Strategic Flow audit deliver? +

Every audit scores your email against the 7-point Strategic Flow diagnostic — subject line, preview text, hook, visual hierarchy, before/after contrast, social proof, and CTA language. You receive a score, a named failure pattern, and a rebuilt version in HTML ready to paste into your ESP. Not suggestions. A finished email.

How is this different from hiring a copywriter? +

Copywriters work from a brief. Strategic Flow works from behavioral data. The audit diagnoses where in the reader's decision journey the email loses them — before a word of copy is changed. Most copywriters improve tone. Strategic Flow fixes architecture. Your copywriter writes better with the right diagnosis.

Does this work with our ESP? +

Yes. Every rebuild outputs HTML compatible with HubSpot, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, Klaviyo, Customer.io, Intercom, and every major ESP. No native integration, no engineering involvement. You receive the HTML — you paste it in. Your existing workflow stays intact.

We only send 2–3 emails per month. Is this worth it? +

Low send frequency usually means higher stakes per email. If one of those emails is your onboarding sequence or a product launch update, a single structural fix — subject line, hook, CTA — can recover the activation the email was losing every month. Run the free diagnostic first. If the score is below 5, the fix pays for itself.

What types of emails does Strategic Flow audit? +

Product update emails, onboarding sequences, changelog announcements, lifecycle emails, and newsletter issues. Strategic Flow also audits LinkedIn product posts and release notes. Any recurring communication where the reader needs to decide whether to act — that's the audit surface.

What does the Single audit include for $49? +

One full 7-point diagnostic, a named failure pattern, the rebuilt email in HTML, and 3 subject line variants with predicted open rate delta. Everything the monthly plans include — for a single send. The right entry point if you have one high-stakes email before a launch or a board meeting.

Can I cancel a monthly plan at any time? +

Cancel any time before the next billing cycle. No lock-in, no cancellation fee. Every rebuilt HTML file, audit report, and subject line variant you received stays with you. The work doesn't disappear when the plan ends.
